# Commercial packaging

## Positioning

QUANTAURA sells **authorization before execution** for AI agents — not another observability dashboard.

## Tiers (indicative)

| Tier | What you get | Who |
|------|----------------|-----|
| **Open Source** | Full MIT kernel, self-host, audit CSV/JSON | Individuals, startups validating |
| **Pro** | Hosted multi-tenant API, retention, email support | Product teams with agents in staging/prod |
| **Enterprise** | SSO, custom roles/timeouts, on-prem, SLA, SOC2 path | Regulated / multi-BU |

Pricing is set per design-partner discussion until public list rates ship.
